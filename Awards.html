Awards & Recognition

Selected research funding, scholarships, presentation honors, and travel awards recognizing my work in musculoskeletal biology, skeletal development, and disease.

Research Funding

  • NIH K99/R00 Award — “Mechanoepigenetic Regulation of Joint Anatomy and Disease” (awarded September 2026).
  • Center for Skeletal Research Mini-Grant — $10,000 to support phenotyping of GDF5 knock-in mice (2024).
  • BMCRC Pilot Fund — $25,000 (awarded July 2023).
  • Harvard Dean’s Competitive Fund Award — $64,000 to support an independent project on energy balance and adolescent long-bone growth (awarded December 2022).
  • NIH BUMC Award — $10,000 to develop spatial transcriptomics approaches for ossified tissues (2023).
  • University College London Proof-of-Concept Award — Co-investigator; £21,500, in collaboration with Diamond Light Source, for cryoSIM and cryoSXT imaging of primary cilia in primary cells (2020). Funded in part by the MRC/EPSRC/BBSRC ImagingBioPro grant (MR/R025673/1).

Scholarships & Entrepreneurship

  • ITInnovations Oxford Grant — £15,000 to co-found Wattson Blue, a mental health and performance platform developed with the University of Oxford Boat Club (2018). The platform is now used as a research tool by other universities.
  • Eliahou Dangoor Scholarship — £1,000 undergraduate scholarship.
