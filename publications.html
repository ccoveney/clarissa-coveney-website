My research integrates mechanobiology, evolutionary biology, functional genomics, and epigenetics to understand musculoskeletal development, skeletal morphology, and disease. I have published 13 peer-reviewed articles, including 6 first-author papers, and currently have 1 first-author preprint, 1 manuscript under review, and 1 additional first-author manuscript in preparation.
